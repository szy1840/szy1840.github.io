lede: "A startup is a way to test and prove your understanding of the world. Success is a byproduct of understanding, and money is a byproduct of the startup."
---

One of my (the?) most important goals in life is to understand the world, society, and people. A startup is one way to test and prove that understanding. A successful startup is a byproduct of the understanding. Making money is a byproduct of the startup.

Stocks and a job are also ways to test and prove your understanding, of course. The difference is how much risk you take.
